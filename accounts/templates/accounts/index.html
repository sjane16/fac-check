About Fac-Check

Fac-Check helps students, faculty, and staff at Universidad de Manila report facility concerns and follow each one until it's fixed.

How it works

  1. 1

    Snap a photo

    Upload a picture of the problem and tell us where it is.

  2. 2

    Auto-sorted, then verified

    An image classifier suggests a category, and the Maintenance and Engineering Division confirms it.

  3. 3

    Track and rate

    Get email updates on every status change, then rate the fix.

Where your report goes

Every report moves through the same four statuses, and you get an email at each change.

  1. Pending We've received your report. Awaiting review
  2. In Progress Maintenance is working on it. Maintenance
  3. Resolved The work is done. Maintenance
  4. Closed You rate the fix, and the report closes. You
